##### People, especially in free software circles, discount the value of good engineering #####

This note is a reflection of lessons I learnt from trying to be part of [Prav](https://prav.app/) project and realizing how it is failing in ways that could have been avoided.

For context, I have [written about Prav previously](https://asd.learnlearn.in/prav-project/) and stated how it had the ambitious goal of simultaneously solving decentralized governance, decentralized messaging, and democratizing these ideas. I specifically wrote:
> Do you want to fight a losing battle against capitalism and concentration of power? Prav Project might be for you.

I knew Prav would not take off when writing that. But I hadn't expected that some of that would be coming from having no respect for the field of engineering.
