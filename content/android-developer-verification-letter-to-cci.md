Google recently announced that it'll be forcing developers to register with government identity cards on their platform to be able to distribute Android applications anywhere. I came across [keepandroidopen.org](https://keepandroidopen.org/) in [FSMK's chat group](https://fsmk.org/) today. So I quickly composed an email to Competition Commission of India. Here's the mail that I sent.

---
Sub: Google is abusing market monopoly to force developers to use its platform
To: cci‑chairman@nic.in

Dear CCI Chairman,

I'm a hobby software developer and also an advocate for software freedom. 

With shock I came to know about Google forcing all developers who are developing applications for Android to be using their platform alone https://developer.android.com/developer-verification

This will prevent third party stores like f-droid from distributing applications. Re: https://f-droid.org/2025/09/29/google-developer-registration-decree.html

This is extremely harmful for the freedom of android users as well as the competition that's allowed against Google. Google is already pushing a lot of other restrictions on Android through its OEM agreements. You have taken note of this earlier, for example in https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2123289

Please also investigate this "Developer Registration" program and consider intervening.

More details are in https://developer.android.com/developer-verification/guides and in https://developer.android.com/developer-verification/assets/pdfs/introducing-the-android-developer-console.pdf

Akshay
